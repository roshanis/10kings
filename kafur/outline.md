# The Right Hand: Outline

A working plan for a novel of about 100,000 words: 24 chapters and a coda, each chapter about 4,000 words. Chapter 1 is drafted.

## How the book moves

Two clocks run through the book, and they alternate chapter by chapter.

- **Now:** Siri, 5 January to about 8 February 1316.
  - Each night Kafur dictates to the scribe Iqbal, for the boy-sultan Shihabuddin Umar.
  - Every Now chapter moves the regency one step closer to the night the paiks come.
- **Then:** 1299 to 4 January 1316.
  - Kafur tells his life in order, from the slave house at Khambhat to the Sultan's deathbed.

The clocks meet at the end. The last Then chapter is the night Alauddin died, and the last Now chapter is the night Kafur dies.

## The record in brief

These dates were checked against modern summaries of the chronicles, not against the chronicles themselves. Check each one against the sources in the README before drafting the chapter that uses it.

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| July 1296 | Alauddin has his uncle, Sultan Jalaluddin, killed on the riverbank at Kara, then marches on Delhi throwing gold to the crowds |
| 1299 | Alauddin's generals sack Gujarat. At Khambhat, Nusrat Khan takes Kafur, a eunuch slave whose master had paid 1,000 dinars for him |
| 1299 | Zafar Khan is killed at Kili after charging the Mongols without orders |
| 1301 | The year of the nephews: Akat Khan's ambush at Tilpat; Umar and Mangu blinded at Ranthambore; Haji Maula's rising in Delhi; Nusrat Khan killed at the siege; Ranthambore taken |
| 1303 | Chittor taken after an eight-month siege; Amir Khusrau records 30,000 killed. Targhi's Mongols besiege Delhi for two months; Siri begun |
| after 1303 | The market and price controls |
| 1305 | The Mongols are beaten at Amroha; the captives are trampled by elephants and their heads displayed at Siri |
| 1306 | Kafur, as *barbeg*, commands against the Mongols on the Ravi, with Malik Tughluq in the vanguard |
| 1307-08 | Kafur takes Devagiri and brings Ramachandra and his family to Delhi |
| 1309-10 | Warangal: Prataparudra submits to Kafur |
| 1311 | Dwarasamudra submits in February; Kafur raids the Pandya country and Madurai in March and April, then returns to Delhi in October |
| 1311 | The massacre of the "New Muslims"; the Alai Darwaza finished; the Alai Minar begun |
| 1313-15 | Kafur kills Singhana III, annexes Devagiri and governs it |
| 1315 | The Sultan falls ill and Kafur is recalled. Alp Khan is executed in December; Khizr Khan is imprisoned at Gwalior |
| 4 Jan 1316 | Alauddin dies. Barani repeats the charge that Kafur hastened his end |
| 5 Jan 1316 | Umar, aged six, is enthroned with Kafur as regent. Malik Sumbul is sent to blind Khizr Khan and Shadi Khan; the Malika-i-Jahan is imprisoned |
| Jan 1316 | Kafur marries Umar's mother, Jhatyapali. Only Firishta, writing about 1607, says so |
| c. 8 Feb 1316 | Kafur sends men to blind Mubarak Khan. Mubarak turns them, and Alauddin's paiks kill Kafur about 35 days after the Sultan's death |
| 1316 | Mubarak becomes regent, blinds Umar, and is Sultan from 14 April |
| 1318 | Mubarak has the blind princes at Gwalior killed, Umar among them |
| 9 July 1320 | Mubarak is murdered by his favourite, Khusrau Khan |
| Sept 1320 | Ghazi Malik Tughluq, who rode in Kafur's vanguard in 1306, overthrows Khusrau Khan and ends the dynasty |

## Part One: The Thousand Dinars

### 1. Camphor (drafted)

*Now: 4-5 January 1316*

- **What happens:**
  - the washing of the Sultan's body;
  - the burial before dawn;
  - the six-year-old enthroned;
  - Malik Sumbul sent to Gwalior.
- **The turn:** "The third keeps pigeons. The third can wait."

### 2. Khambhat

*Then: 1299*

- **What happens:**
  - Kafur's boyhood in a rich *khwaja*'s house in the port: indigo vats, carnelian workshops, ships waiting on the tide in the gulf.
  - The thousand dinars. His castration, told once and briefly, never mentioned again.
  - Ulugh Khan and Nusrat Khan sweep Gujarat and sack Somnath. Nusrat Khan strips the merchants of Khambhat and takes Kafur.
  - The conversion and the new name.
  - The march north: the Mongol converts mutiny over the loot at Jalore, and their families are punished.
- **The turn:** Kafur learns that he is loot, and that loot is policy.
- **Record:** the capture at Khambhat, the thousand dinars, the conversion; the Jalore mutiny (Barani).
- **Invented:** his family, his birthplace, the khwaja, when and how he was cut.

### 3. The Oath

*Now: days 2-4*

- **What happens:**
  - The amirs swear again, in council.
  - Kafur takes stock:
    - the treasury;
    - who commands where (Malik Tughluq at Dipalpur, Alp Khan's leaderless men in Gujarat).
  - The Malika-i-Jahan is confined, and her treasure taken.
  - The boy asks where his brothers are.
- **The turn:** Kafur sees that the amirs are waiting, not obeying.
- **Record:** the Malika-i-Jahan's imprisonment.
- **Invented:** the council.

### 4. Gold Stars

*Then: 1299-1300*

- **What happens:**
  - Nusrat Khan presents Kafur to the Sultan. In their first meeting the Sultan asks him the price of things, and he knows them.
  - The court tells Kafur its founding story:
    - the Devagiri raid of 1296 that paid for the coup;
    - Kara;
    - the march on Delhi, with gold flung from a mangonel at every halt.
  - Kili: Zafar Khan dies after charging without orders. The Mongols say that when their horses would not drink, they asked them, "Did you see Zafar Khan?"
  - The Sultan does not mourn a hero who was too popular to live.
- **The turn:** Kafur sees how the Sultan measures men: by use.
- **Record:**
  - Isami: the Sultan favoured Kafur because his counsel always proved fit for the occasion.
  - Barani: the gold and Kili.
- **Invented:** the first meeting.

### 5. Gwalior

*Now: days 6-9*

- **What happens:**
  - Sumbul's rider comes back: it is done. Kafur's reaction shows in his body, not in words.
  - Khizr Khan as Kafur knew him:
    - the heir who loved a captive princess, Deval Devi, the subject of Amir Khusrau's poem;
    - by later accounts, a disciple of the Sufi Nizamuddin Auliya (check the chronicles).
  - The boy asks whether his brothers are coming to see him.
- **The turn:** Kafur must choose between the truth and a lie for the boy. Decide which in drafting.
- **Record:** the blinding at Gwalior by Malik Sumbul's men; Khusrau's *Duval Rani Khizr Khan*.
- **Invented:** the report and the scenes.

### 6. Tilpat

*Then: 1301*

- **What happens:** the year of the nephews.
  - Akat Khan's riders ambush the Sultan on a hunt at Tilpat. He lies wounded behind his guards' shields while they proclaim him dead. Akat is caught and beheaded.
  - At Ranthambore, a stone from the walls kills Nusrat Khan, the man who took Kafur at Khambhat.
  - The Sultan's sister's sons, Umar and Mangu, rise in Badaun and Awadh. They are brought to the camp, and their eyes are put out in front of him.
  - In Delhi, Haji Maula seizes the city and crowns a puppet.
  - Ranthambore falls.
- **The turn:** Kafur watches the Sultan blind a nephew named Umar. He tells the boy so.
- **Record:** all of it (Barani).
- **Invented:** Kafur's presence and his part.

### 7. The Widow

*Now: days 10-12*

- **What happens:**
  - Kafur marries Jhatyapali, the boy's mother, and makes himself the Sultan's stepfather.
  - It is a wedding with no wedding night. They bargain over terms, hers and his.
  - What she remembers of Devagiri, and what she wants for her son.
  - She asks about Mubarak.
- **The turn:** she is a better politician than he is, and he knows it.
- **Record:** Firishta alone reports the marriage, three centuries later. Treat it as possible, not certain.
- **Invented:** everything in the room.

### 8. Siri

*Then: 1303*

- **What happens:**
  - Chittor:
    - the eight-month siege;
    - the surrender;
    - the killing of 30,000 people that Amir Khusrau, who was there, records.
  - The Padmini of later legend is not in it. Kafur has never heard of her, because nobody has yet.
  - Home to a Delhi stripped of troops. Targhi's Mongols camp in the suburbs, and the Sultan digs in behind a ditch at Siri for two months until they leave.
  - The humiliation becomes a system:
    - the price regime;
    - the granaries;
    - the market superintendent;
    - the spies;
    - flesh cut from a shopkeeper to the weight he cheated.
- **The turn:** the merchant's slave understands the Sultan's plan before the Sultan has finished explaining it.
- **Record:** Chittor (Khusrau); the siege, Siri and the markets (Barani).
- **Invented:** Kafur's part in the plan.

## Part Two: The South

### 9. Prices

*Now: days 13-15*

- **What happens:**
  - Kafur governs. The markets hold because he makes them hold.
  - Money flows out to the amirs faster than it comes in.
  - Gujarat has been in revolt since Alp Khan's execution.
  - The paiks' pay is late, and Kafur pays it from his own purse.
- **The turn:** for the first time, Kafur cannot make the sums come out.
- **Record:** the Gujarat revolt (verify who was sent against it, and when).
- **Invented:** the detail.

### 10. The Ravi

*Then: 1305-06*

- **What happens:**
  - Amroha: the captives trampled, the heads at Siri.
  - Kafur's first command. In 1306 the Chagatai Mongols under Kopek reach the Ravi, and the Sultan sends his barbeg, a slave, over the heads of the free-born.
  - Malik Tughluq rides in the vanguard.
  - The victory.
- **The turn:** Kafur learns he can win. The reader knows that the man in his vanguard will end this dynasty in 1320.
- **Record:** Kafur's command, Tughluq in the vanguard, and a decisive victory.
- **Invented:** the battle as Kafur sees it from the saddle.

### 11. Pigeons

*Now: days 16-18*

- **What happens:**
  - Kafur visits Mubarak Khan in his rooms. The prince is charming, idle and frightened, and he is watching.
  - The boy Umar loves his brother.
  - Kafur decides, again, that the third can wait.
- **The turn:** the reader sees what Kafur does not: Mubarak is talking to the guards.
- **Record:** Mubarak was confined in the palace.
- **Invented:** the visit.

### 12. Devagiri

*Then: 1307-08*

- **What happens:**
  - The first southern campaign. Ramachandra of Devagiri has stopped paying; Kafur marches, and the king comes out to meet him.
  - Kafur brings Ramachandra and his family to Delhi, among them the daughter who will be the boy's mother.
  - The Sultan honours the old king with:
    - the title *Rai Rayan*, king of kings;
    - a hundred thousand gold *tankas*;
    - Navsari.
  - Alp Khan's men catch Deval Devi, the fugitive princess of Gujarat, who will marry Khizr Khan.
- **The turn:** Kafur learns that mercy to a king buys more than the sack of his city.
- **Record:**
  - Barani: Ramachandra's honours.
  - Isami: Jhatyapali given to the Sultan. Some historians date the marriage to 1296 instead.
- **Invented:** the journey north.

### 13. The Rumour

*Now: days 19-21*

- **What happens:**
  - The grain market's story reaches Siri: Kafur poisoned the Sultan.
  - Kafur traces the story to its source.
  - Iqbal asks the question the reader wants to ask. Kafur does not answer.
- **The turn:** Kafur realises the rumour is not being spread against him. It is being spread for someone.
- **Record:** Barani's later charge.
- **Invented:** the tracing.

### 14. Warangal

*Then: 1309-10*

- **What happens:**
  - The march across the Deccan, through a Devagiri that now supplies Delhi's armies.
  - The siege of Warangal's outer earth wall and inner stone wall.
  - Prataparudra submits: elephants, horses, treasure, and a tribute agreed.
  - Kafur has never heard of a diamond called the Koh-i-Noor. Nobody will call it that for four hundred years.
  - The return to Delhi in the summer of 1310.
- **Record:** Khusrau, *Khaza'in ul-Futuh*.
- **Invented:** the detail.

### 15. The Paiks

*Now: days 22-24*

- **What happens:**
  - Kafur inspects the palace guard. He knows their names.
  - He remembers that paiks shielded the Sultan at Tilpat, and trusts them because they were the Sultan's.
- **The turn:** the reader sees the irony that Kafur cannot.
- **Record:** the paiks as Alauddin's foot guards.
- **Invented:** the scene.

### 16. Madurai

*Then: 1310-11*

- **What happens:**
  - Dwarasamudra: Ballala III submits in February 1311 and guides the army south.
  - The Pandya brothers, Sundara and Vira, are at war over their father's throne.
  - Kafur's raid through the Pandya country:
    - temples stripped, in Khusrau's triumphant account;
    - Madurai found empty;
    - the southern sea.
  - The rains and a gathering Pandya army turn him back with the loot.
- **The turn:** it is the furthest south a Delhi army has ever gone, and Kafur knows it was a raid, not a conquest.
- **Record:** Khusrau and Barani. The claim that Kafur reached Rameswaram comes from later writers and is doubted.
- **Invented:** Kafur's view of the sea, and of the temples.

### 17. The Third Son

*Now: days 25-27*

- **What happens:**
  - Kafur decides that Mubarak must be blinded.
  - Why he waited:
    - contempt;
    - the boy's love for his brother;
    - the Sultan's face in the young man's.
  - Jhatyapali advises him. Decide in drafting whether she helps him or warns Mubarak.
- **The turn:** Kafur chooses the paiks for the task.
- **Record:** Kafur sent men to blind or kill Mubarak.
- **Invented:** the reasons.

### 18. Five Hundred and Twelve Elephants

*Then: 1311*

- **What happens:**
  - The return to Delhi in October and the Sultan's welcome.
  - The counting of the loot:
    - Khusrau, who was there, gives 512 elephants, 5,000 horses and 500 *mans* of gold and jewels.
    - Barani, decades later, gives 612 elephants, 20,000 horses and 96,000 mans.
  - Kafur, who counted, corrects the record.
  - The same year:
    - the massacre of the New Muslims, after a plot to kill the Sultan on a hawking trip;
    - the Alai Darwaza finished;
    - the tower begun.
  - Barani says the Sultan was infatuated with Kafur.
- **The turn:** this is the summit. Everything after it is descent.
- **Record:** Khusrau's and Barani's figures; the massacre (Barani); the gate's 1311 inscription.
- **Invented:** Kafur's count.

## Part Three: The Sickroom

### 19. Gujarat

*Now: days 28-30*

- **What happens:**
  - The empire cracks at the edges: revolts reported, amirs pressing.
  - Malik Tughluq writes from Dipalpur.
  - Kafur has never had fewer friends or more power.
- **Record:** the revolts after Alp Khan's death.
- **Invented:** Tughluq's letter.

### 20. Devagiri Again

*Then: 1313-15*

- **What happens:**
  - Singhana III defies Delhi. Kafur kills him and annexes the kingdom.
  - Two years governing from Devagiri, far from the Sultan.
  - The Sultan's letters.
  - Alp Khan's rise at court.
  - The recall in 1315.
- **Record:** the annexation and the governorship.
- **Invented:** the letters.

### 21. The Order

*Now: days 31-33*

- **What happens:**
  - Kafur sends the paiks to Mubarak's rooms at dusk.
  - They come back and say it is done. It is not.
- **Record:** Mubarak turned the men sent to blind him.
- **Invented:** how Kafur is deceived. Check Barani and Isami for what Mubarak gave the men and what he said to them.

### 22. The Sickroom

*Then: 1315*

- **What happens:**
  - The Sultan's decline.
  - Alp Khan executed in December on a charge that he, his sister and Khizr Khan plotted to poison the Sultan. The charge may have been Kafur's own story.
  - Khizr Khan sent to Gwalior.
  - The council at the bedside, and the will naming Umar.
- **Record:** Barani. Modern historians suspect Kafur's hand.
- **Invented:** the sickroom.

### 23. The Last Night

*Then: 4 January 1316*

- **What happens:** the night the Sultan died. Kafur keeps his promise and tells the boy what happened.
- **The choice:** this is the book's central decision. The recommendation is that Kafur tells the whole truth as he saw it, and that it still leaves room for doubt.

### 24. The Door

*Now: about 8 February 1316*

- **What happens:**
  - Kafur dictates his last pages.
  - He knows, or half-knows. The torch passes from hand to hand, as it did on the road to the grave.

### Coda: Iqbal

- **What happens:** the scribe writes one page:
  - the paiks at the door;
  - the body;
  - the grave nobody marked.
  - Iqbal kept the pages for the Sultan Umar, to give him when he was grown. In the spring they blinded him.

### Historical Note

- What is recorded, and what is invented.
- What became of everyone:
  - The paiks freed Mubarak, and he dispersed them once he was Sultan.
  - He blinded Umar, and in 1318 had the blind princes at Gwalior killed.
  - He was murdered in 1320 by Khusrau Khan.
  - Khusrau Khan fell the same year to Ghazi Malik Tughluq.
