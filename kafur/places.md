# The Right Hand: Places

The main settings follow the setting sketch in `Notes/19 Setting Sketch`.

## Siri

- **Location:** Delhi, north-east of the old city, about an hour's walk from the Jami mosque.
- **Role in Story:**
  - The stage of every Now chapter.
  - The palace where the Sultan dies, where Kafur dictates, and where the paiks come for him.
- **Related Characters:**
  - Kafur;
  - Umar;
  - Jhatyapali;
  - Mubarak Khan, confined three rooms from Kafur;
  - the Malika-i-Jahan;
  - Iqbal;
  - the paiks.
- **Season:**
  - January 1316: frost on the lawns at dawn, fog at night, and the smoke of dung fires lying over the city like a second roof.
  - By early February: warmer days, cold nights.
- **Unique Features:**
  - Alauddin began Siri in 1303, when the Mongols besieged Delhi and he had to dig in behind a ditch.
  - After Amroha (1305), the heads of the Mongol captives were displayed there.
  - The palace had audience halls and gardens with water channels.
  - Sources place the Palace of a Thousand Pillars inside or beside Siri. Settle this before naming it on the page.
- **Description:** a new city: a garrison, a palace and a warning in one.
- **Sights:**
  - paiks at every door, bare-legged, shields slung on their backs;
  - braziers;
  - carpets over cold stone;
  - torches in the fog.
- **Sounds:**
  - jackals in the ruins of the old camps;
  - the drums of the watch;
  - the call to prayer;
  - Mubarak's pigeons.
- **Smell:**
  - camphor in chapter 1;
  - lamp oil;
  - dung smoke;
  - wet stone.
- **Notes:** a few stretches of Siri's walls survive in south Delhi near Shahpur Jat. Walk them before drafting.

## The Jami mosque and the Sultan's tomb

- **Location:** the old city (Qila Rai Pithora), at what is now the Qutb Minar complex.
- **Role in Story:**
  - The funeral prayer and the burial before dawn (chapter 1).
  - The iron pillar Kafur touches whenever he passes it.
- **Related Characters:** Kafur, the amirs, and the dead Sultan.
- **Season:** a January night, then the dawn prayer.
- **Unique Features:**
  - The mosque was built from the pillars of 27 temples, as the inscription over its eastern gate says.
  - In the courtyard stands an iron pillar of the fourth or fifth century that does not rust.
  - The Qutb Minar, "the old tower".
  - Alauddin's red and white gate, the Alai Darwaza, finished in 1311.
  - The Alai Minar, begun in 1311 to be twice the Qutb's height and abandoned at his death as a rubble drum about 25 metres high, never faced with stone.
  - The madrasa of 1315, where Alauddin was buried.
- **Description:** the oldest thing the Sultans built in Delhi, and built out of other people's things.
- **Sights:**
  - torchlight on reused temple pillars;
  - the tower invisible in fog;
  - the stump of the new tower.
- **Sounds:** four *Allahu akbar* for the dead, then earth on the shroud, then the call to the dawn prayer.
- **Smell:** camphor from the shroud; cold stone.
- **Notes:** the tomb's dome has fallen. Describe it as it was.

## Khambhat

- **Location:** the head of the Gulf of Khambhat, Gujarat.
- **Role in Story:**
  - Kafur's childhood.
  - The sack of 1299, and his capture (chapter 2).
- **Related Characters:**
  - Kafur;
  - the khwaja who bought him;
  - Kafur's mother;
  - Nusrat Khan.
- **Season:** 1299. Check the month of the sack against the campaign's dates.
- **Unique Features:**
  - One of the great ports of the Indian Ocean, trading with the Gulf and Arabia.
  - Indigo, cotton cloth, and the carnelian and agate beads its workshops polished by the thousand.
  - Huge tides and a tidal bore, with ships sitting on the mud at low water.
  - Marco Polo described it as "Cambaet" a few years before the sack.
- **Description:** rich, crowded and many-tongued. Muslim and Hindu merchants live in great houses above the creek.
- **Sights:**
  - blue-stained hands at the indigo vats;
  - red stones on the bead-drillers' spindles;
  - ships heeled over on the mud.
- **Sounds:**
  - the tide coming in fast;
  - Arabic, Persian and Gujarati in the same street;
  - the bead-drills.
- **Smell:**
  - the rot of fermenting indigo;
  - salt mud;
  - cotton bales;
  - pepper.
- **Notes:** read Marco Polo's chapter on Cambaet, and check Khusrau and Barani for the sack itself.

## Gwalior

- **Location:** a rock plateau about five days' hard ride south of Delhi.
- **Role in Story:**
  - The state prison.
  - Khizr Khan and Shadi Khan are blinded there (chapter 5), and later Umar is sent there.
  - Kafur never goes there in the novel; the book looks at it from Siri, the way a man avoids looking at a wound.
- **Related Characters:**
  - Khizr Khan;
  - Shadi Khan;
  - Malik Sumbul;
  - later, Umar.
- **Season:** January 1316.
- **Unique Features:**
  - Sheer cliffs.
  - The old temples on the plateau: the Teli ka Mandir and the Sas-Bahu temples were already old in 1316.
  - The Man Singh palace does not exist yet.
- **Description:** the place where princes go to be forgotten.
- **Sights, sounds, smell:** seen only through Sumbul's report. Keep it spare.
- **Notes:** check which gates and buildings existed in 1316 before describing any of them.
